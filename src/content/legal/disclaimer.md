## This page is information, not advice

These pages are educational material only. They are not a diagnosis, not an
assessment, and not a substitute for being seen by a physiotherapist.

## It is a reminder, not a starting point

Exercises are prescribed individually. What you see here is a reminder of what
your physiotherapist has already prescribed for you in person, so you can
follow it correctly at home. It is not a programme to begin on your own.

Do only the items your physiotherapist pointed you to, at the numbers they
gave you. If you were not given an exercise, it is not for you.

## Stop, and contact the clinic

Stop straight away and contact the clinic if you notice any of the following:

- pain that is new, sharp, or getting worse
- dizziness or light-headedness
- numbness, pins and needles, or weakness
- shortness of breath
- anything that does not feel right to you

Mild muscle effort is expected. Pain is not.

## In an emergency

If you have chest pain, cannot breathe, collapse, lose consciousness, or lose
control of your bladder or bowel, do not wait and do not use this page. Call the
emergency services immediately.

## What this material does not cover

This material is general to a body area. It cannot take account of your
individual circumstances — your history, other conditions you may have,
medication you take, whether you are pregnant, or any recent surgery or injury.
Only the physiotherapist who assessed you can judge whether an exercise suits
you, and their instruction to you takes priority over anything written here.
