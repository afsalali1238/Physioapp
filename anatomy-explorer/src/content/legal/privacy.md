## Nothing about you is collected

There is no sign-in, no account, and no form on this site. You are not asked for
your name, your date of birth, or any detail about your health. Nothing you do
here is recorded against you.

## No analytics, no tracking

There are no analytics, no advertising tags, no session recording, and no
third-party scripts of any kind. Nobody is counting your visits or watching
which exercises you open.

## What is stored on your own device

Two small preferences are saved by your browser, on your device only:

- the text size you chose
- which items you ticked as done today

These never leave your device and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your
browser data removes them. Ticking an item is for your own benefit — no one at
the clinic can see it.

## Where the pages come from

The pages and images are served by our hosting provider. Fonts and images are
served from the same place as the pages themselves, so opening a page does not
reveal your visit to any other company.

Like any website, our host processes the technical information your browser
sends in order to deliver the page, including your IP address. This is used to
serve the page and for security, not to build a profile of you.
