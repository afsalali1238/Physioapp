Review all patient-facing copy for clinical safety. Use the `clinical-copy-reviewer` subagent.

Scope: every string a patient can read — screens, buttons, labels, empty states, error text,
aria-labels and live-region announcements.

Check for: condition or injury names · cause or likelihood language · outcome, recovery or
timescale claims · superlatives · booking or contact CTAs · reassurance the app is not qualified
to give · alarm conveyed by colour or motion alone · anything a frightened person would misread.

Report findings by severity with the exact string and a suggested replacement. Do not change
clinical wording yourself — flag it.
