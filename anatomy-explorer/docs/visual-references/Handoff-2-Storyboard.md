## Assumptions & Questions for Physiotherapist
1. **Shoulder Position:** I have assumed the shoulders remain entirely neutral and relaxed during this movement. Should we explicitly highlight or instruct keeping the shoulders down?
2. **Return Speed:** The return to neutral is currently timed to be as controlled as the tuck. Is it clinically preferable to return slowly, or just relax immediately?
3. **Neck Angle:** The movement is strictly horizontal translation (straight back). Does the patient tend to tilt their head up or down incorrectly? If so, should the animation or text emphasize keeping the gaze level?
